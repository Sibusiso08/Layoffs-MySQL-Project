# 🚲 Bike Sales Analysis – Excel Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
Analyzed demographic data to identify key factors that influence bicycle purchases. Built an interactive Excel dashboard to help sales and marketing teams optimize targeting and strategy.

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Charts, Slicers, VLOOKUP)

## 🧾 Dataset Features
- Gender, Age, Marital Status, Education, Income, Region
- Purchase decision (Yes/No)

## 🔍 Key Business Questions
1. Which customer segments are more likely to purchase bikes?
2. How do demographics like income or education influence buying behavior?
3. Are there regional or occupational trends?

## 🔬 What I Did
- Cleaned the raw data
- Created calculated fields (e.g., income brackets)
- Built pivot tables to analyze purchase behavior by demographics
- Visualized key trends in a dashboard with filters

## 💡 Key Insights
- High-income, single, urban males aged 30–45 are the most likely to purchase
- College-educated customers had the highest purchase rates
- The Pacific region leads in sales conversion

## 📈 Business Impact
- Better targeted marketing strategies
- Informed regional ad budget allocation
- Strategic segmentation for product bundles
